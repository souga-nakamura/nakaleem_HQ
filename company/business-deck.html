Nakaleem Industries · Business Overview 03 / 10
Flagship · Real Estate

Burjan — beat Zameen on the deal, not on ads

Not a listing-ad portal. Burjan acts as the broker in the deal itself. Listings are free to build inventory; we earn on closed transactions.

Commission model (both sides)

DealOwner / SellerTenant / BuyerTotal
Rental0.5 month0.5 month1 month's rent
Sale0.5%0.5%1.0% of price

Rentals drive volume; sales drive value (a single sale ≈ several to many rentals).

How we win

  • Own the brokerage in-house — our own salaried agents, led directly by the COO.
  • Density first: dominate one area (Lahore DHA / Bahria) before expanding.
  • Different revenue model from Zameen — no head-to-head ad war.
  • Quality, customers and data compound inside the company.

Shared Workspace

ShareLounge — the space between café and office

A premium, all-open lounge in DHA Phase 3. Deliberately not a time-metered co-working desk farm — a place people want to be.

Format

~220 m² · ~22–24 seats, all open. No fixed desks, no meeting rooms. Open 8:00–22:00.

Pricing

Day pass 2,500 PKR · Membership 40,000–45,000 PKR/mo. Fully cashless (card + QR).

Experience

"Evening library" mood — dark wood, greenery, layered warm light. F&B is a paid, on-brand revenue line.

Fit-out budget ≈ PKR 4M Pre-open: late August Grand opening: September 1
Nakaleem Industries · Business Overview 05 / 10
Specialty Coffee

Coffee Shop — a quality-led coffee house

Benchmarked on Yoneda Coffee: easy to walk into, comfortable to linger, and you can genuinely eat well — not a vague "café".

Concept

30–40 seats, wood & modern. Families & locals. Signature: made-to-order pancakes.

Operations

Table-side tablet ordering — lifts spend and cuts labour without touching barista quality.

Unit economics

~3.78M PKR / mo base revenue

Avg ticket ~1,300 PKR · EBITDA ~32–34% · payback ~12 months.

Investment

25M PKR

Fit-out + working capital + reserve. Not "all-in" on one site.

Concept locks in August · site secured and fit-out started within September.

Dining · Succession

Zanzibar — inherit a brand, then grow it

An established Fine-Dining & Buffet name in Multan, succeeded from family at no acquisition cost. We keep the team and the fit-out, and lift profitability.

The asset

4.2★

2,870 Google reviews · 4,630+ Facebook followers. A genuine local powerhouse.

The mission

Owner take-home ~1M → 3–5M PKR/mo. Not just "keep it running" — improve the economics.

The levers

Ticket, cost ratio, utilisation, ops — where the CEO's menu & branding strength pays off.

Long-term vision: convert to — and roll out — an own-brand Sizzler-style chain within 3 years.
